# Telco-Customer-Churn
The goal is to predict if the customers will leave the bank
#Dataset
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn
#Variables: 
customerID, gender, SeniorCitizen, Partner, Dependents, tenure, PhoneService, MultipleLines, InternetService, OnlineSecurity, OnlineBackup, DeviceProtection, TechSupport, StreamingTV, StreamingMovies, Contract, PaperlessBilling, PaymentMethod, MonthlyCharges, TotalCharges, Churn  
#Outliers and NAs
No NAs and few outliers
#Insights: 
- Random Forest can predict 83% of churn
- Demographic analysis: Clients with partners or dependents have lower churn rates, while senior citizens have higher churn rates
- Contract analysis: Month-to-month contracts, electronic check and paperless billing are more likely to churn
- Numeric variables analysis: Customers with higher tenure, lower monthly charges or higher total charges are prone to stay at the bank
- Service analysis: customers with fiber optic, no online security no online backup, no tech support, no device protection have higher leave rate, while customers with no internet service have lower churn.
